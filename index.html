Method

Four steps, every batch, no shortcuts

The same sequence runs on every lot that comes through the door, so what changes is the bean, not the process.

01

Cup it blind

Every green lot is cupped before we commit to buying it — no origin story overrides a bad cup.

02

Roast in small batches

12kg at a time on a drum roaster, profiled per lot rather than run on a house curve.

03

Rest and re-cup

Beans rest for 5–10 days, then we cup again to confirm the profile before it ships.

04

Ship within 48 hours

Bagged and shipped the same week it's roasted — never from a warehouse shelf.
